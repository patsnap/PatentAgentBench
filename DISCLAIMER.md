## Scope and purpose

PatentAgentBench (PAB) provides benchmark datasets, reference annotations, evaluation criteria, and supporting materials for researching and evaluating AI systems on professional patent tasks. It includes Claim Charting Bench, Design FTO Bench, FTO Search Bench, Novelty Search Bench, Patent Drafting Bench, Patent Invalidity Search Bench, Patent Translation Bench, and Office Action Response Bench (OAR).

This disclaimer covers the published PAB records, patent texts, images, Office Action documents, technical disclosures, annotations, reference answers, rubrics, and documentation. References to model outputs or evaluation results describe results produced by users or third-party systems using PAB; publication of the benchmark does not itself provide a model or certify those results.

## Limits of the data and evaluation

PAB materials and results obtained using them may contain errors, omissions, inconsistent annotations, incomplete evidence, translation mistakes, or outdated information. Patent documents and case materials reflect particular dates and jurisdictions; their inclusion does not establish current legal status or current applicable law.

Retrieval reference sets identify annotated references rather than every potentially relevant patent or prior-art document. Failure to retrieve or annotate a patent does not establish that no relevant patent exists or that a product is free to operate. Feature alignments, disclosure labels, drafting rubrics, and Office Action response criteria are evaluation references, not authoritative legal findings or universally applicable answers.

Benchmark scores depend on the selected samples, reference sets, matching rules, retrieval access, judge configuration, and aggregation method. They do not certify performance on other technologies, languages, jurisdictions, or real-world matters.
